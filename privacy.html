⚽ Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

Welcome to KoraSim.com ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our FIFA World Cup 2026 simulator.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to provide our service:

  • Usage Data — Pages visited, time spent, device type, browser, and country (via Vercel Analytics). This data is anonymous and aggregated.
  • Prediction Data — Your group standings, bracket picks, and favorite team are stored only in your browser's local storage. We do not store this on our servers.
  • Share Links — When you share a prediction link, the data is encoded in the URL itself. We do not store shared predictions on our servers.

2. Cookies

KoraSim does not use tracking cookies. We use localStorage (browser storage) to save your predictions locally on your device. This data never leaves your browser unless you share it via a link.

3. Analytics

We use Vercel Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. This service collects anonymous, aggregated data such as:

  • Number of page views
  • Country of origin (not precise location)
  • Device type and browser

No personal information is collected. No cookies are set by our analytics. For more information, see Vercel's Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising

We may display advertisements via Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

For more information on how Google uses data, please visit Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Third-Party Services

Our site uses the following third-party services:

  • Vercel — Hosting and analytics (Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdSense — Advertising (Privacy Policy)
  • Google Fonts — Typography (Privacy Policy)
  • flag-icons CDN — Country flag images

6. Data Retention

Since we do not collect personal data on our servers, there is nothing to retain or delete. Your prediction data is stored only in your browser and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser's local storage or clicking the "Reset" button on our site.

7. Children's Privacy

KoraSim is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

8. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, there is nothing to access, modify, or delete on our end. You can clear your local data at any time through your browser settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
